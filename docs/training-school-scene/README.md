# Training School scene

The Training School is an outdoor field on the same 640 × 680 stage as the park. It is the `school` entry in `src/actor/scenes.ts`, drawn by the same sky, grass, weather, and prop painters as the park.

- Live sky and weather, blue-green grass (the park stays yellow-green), and blowing leaves (ten, drifted the same way as the park's twelve).
- A fence, chalk field lines, a goal with pennants, trees, bushes, and a bench.
- Birds: two flocks on the grass, one perched on the goal, and one on the bench.
- The pet's home spot is the middle of the field, about (330, 470). The walkable grass runs from y 214 to y 654.
- Podiums, tunnels, hurdles, hoops, and cones are not painted. The player places them, and they leave when the pet changes rooms.

The SVG files in this folder are an earlier indoor painting. The app does not draw them.
