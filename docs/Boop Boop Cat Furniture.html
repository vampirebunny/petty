    Every length scales with the cat's own body, so the same code works for every breed, age and weight. Scene units: the adult torso is 58 px and the room is 900 × 600.

    Changed from the brief

    • Stroke pull is 18% of front leg length, up from 15%. At phone size 15% barely reads.
    • Jump range is 110 px × age factor, with kitten 0.75 and senior 0.85 (the brief said 0.6 and 0.5). The lower values left the senior unable to reach even the lowest platform. The kitten still can't make the first jump, so it climbs the post.
    • Perch holds are 5–9 s and the furniture cooldown is 20 s so the demo moves along. Keep 8–25 s and 3 minutes in the app.
    • Platforms are staggered left and right so every jump has a clear arc. Future trees and shelves need the same zig-zag or the jump graph has no edges.
