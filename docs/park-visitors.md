# Park visitors

On the home park, other animals may wander through. The living room, bedroom, catio, shop, and playdates stay as they are. A playdate is still the visit you plan. A park visitor is just someone passing through.

## Who shows up

Zero, one, or two visitors can be on screen. Three never overlap.

Arriving at the park rolls about 50% empty, 35% one visitor, and 15% two. While you stay, the park checks every 20–35 seconds. If it is empty, someone enters about 45% of the time. If one visitor is already there, another enters about 20% of the time. At two, nobody else enters.

A visit lasts 30–75 seconds and never past about 90 seconds. The animal then walks off the nearer edge. Leaving the park removes whoever is still there. They do not keep a place on the grass.

About 70% of visitors are animals from the owner's playdate cast. The rest are one-off strangers drawn from the same names and breeds. Any species, any breed. The same animal is not in both slots, and does not enter twice in a row.

They enter from the left or the right, whichever is farther from your pet, at least about 120px from your pet and from the other visitor. Reduced motion still walks them on and off. It does not add zoomies.

## Sociability

There is no separate sociability stat. Boldness is whether someone walks up. Affection is whether they want to be friendly. Both are the shown scores from the Traits panel, the same scale as playdate shyness (boldness under 40).

| Band | Scores | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Shy | Boldness under 40, affection at least 40 | Does not cross the park. Accepts a hello only if the other animal is already within about 70px. |
| Aloof | Boldness at least 40, affection under 40 | May walk over, then only sniffs. |
| Avoids | Both under 40 | Nobody approaches. If someone comes within about 45px, they step aside, and that visitor leaves a little sooner. |
| Friendly | Both at least 40 | May walk over while idle. |
| Outgoing | Both at least 65 | Seeks visitors. Two outgoing dogs who are not puppies or seniors may run up to each other. |

Energy under 35 blocks a cross-park walk. That animal only answers if the other one is already close. Mood under 25 multiplies the chance to start a hello by 0.4. Asleep, eating, drinking, chasing, cuddling, traveling, or doing something you asked ignores visitors.

A hello starts only when both sides allow it.

- If either side avoids company, nobody walks over.
- If one wants to meet and the other will at least tolerate it, the interested one walks to a meet point and stops about 36px short (about 52px for a dog and a cat).
- If both would only tolerate a hello, they do not cross the park. They can still greet when a wander brings them within about 70px.
- If both want to meet, they meet halfway.

Your pet and the visitor each roll about every 8 seconds while idle. Outgoing starts a hello about 55% of the time, friendly or aloof about 28%, and shy never starts one. One hello per visitor. Two visitors may greet each other once if your pet is sitting it out. That greeting does not pay mood. A park-find prompt holds your pet, so a hello does not start until the prompt is gone.

The hello lasts 2–6 seconds, then both go back to wandering. A dog and a dog may bow. A cat and a cat may sit. Anyone aloof, or a dog and a cat, sniffs. A player command, including feed, play, sleep, cuddle, or a trick, cancels the hello immediately. There is no mood penalty.

## What it pays

A finished hello between your pet and a visitor gives +2 mood, once per visitor, and at most +6 mood per 10 minutes. Mood still has to be allowed to rise. No attribute points and no bond. Turning away is not a miss.

Visitors do not eat, drink, sleep, or take the toy. Pond drinks and meals stay on your pet.
