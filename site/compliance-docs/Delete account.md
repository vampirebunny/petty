# Account & Data Deletion

### Delete in the App

1. Open Boop Boop and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm. You'll be signed out, and deletion begins.

### Delete Without the App

Email help@mylisto.app from the address on your account, with the subject line "Delete My Boop Boop Account." If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, include your Boop Boop handle instead. We may ask you to verify the request before acting on it.

### Playing Without an Account

If you never created an account, your pet and progress are stored on your device, and deleting the App deletes them. We also keep a limited record tied to an app-generated identifier, such as your age confirmation, your acceptance of our Terms, and any purchase records. Because this record is not linked to an account, we may not be able to locate it from an email request.

### What Gets Deleted

| Data                                                    | What happens                                   | Typical timing                               |
|---------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Account details (handle, email, sign-in ID)             | Deleted                                        | Generally within 30 days                     |
| Pets, progress, and Virtual Items                       | Deleted, and cannot be recovered               | Generally within 30 days                     |
| Friends list, friend requests, and blocked users        | Deleted; you are removed from friends' lists   | Generally within 30 days                     |
| Notification tokens                                     | Deleted                                        | Generally within 30 days                     |
| Diagnostics and usage data                              | Deleted or de-identified                       | On our service providers' retention schedules |
| Backups                                                 | Overwritten on our normal backup cycle         | Generally within 90 days                     |
| Purchase records                                        | Kept for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention | Up to 7 years                                |
| Reports involving safety or legal issues                | Kept where required or permitted by law        | As needed                                    |

These timeframes are typical, not guaranteed. We may keep information where required or permitted by law, for example to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our Terms.

**Before you delete:** deletion is permanent, and purchased Virtual Items are not refunded. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies, so request one first if you plan to. Deleting your account does not cancel purchases in your Apple or Google account.
