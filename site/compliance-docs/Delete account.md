## Account & Data Deletion

### Delete in the App

1. Open Boop Boop and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Account**, then **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm. You'll be signed out, and deletion begins.

### Delete Without the App

Email \[PRIVACY EMAIL\] from the address on your account, with the subject line "Delete My Boop Boop Account." If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, include your Boop Boop username instead. We may ask you to verify the request before acting on it.

### Playing Without an Account

If you never created an account, your pet is stored only on your device, and deleting the App deletes it. You may also email us to request deletion of any diagnostic data associated with your device.

### What Gets Deleted

| Data | What happens | Typical timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account details (email, display name, sign-in ID) | Deleted | Generally within 30 days |
| Pets, progress, and Virtual Items | Deleted, and cannot be recovered | Generally within 30 days |
| Friends list and friend requests | Deleted; you are removed from friends' lists | Generally within 30 days |
| Diagnostics and usage data | Deleted or de-identified | Generally within 30 days |
| Backups | Overwritten on our normal backup cycle | Generally within 90 days |
| Purchase records | Kept for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention | Up to 7 years |
| Reports involving safety or legal issues | Kept where required or permitted by law | As needed |

These timeframes are typical, not guaranteed. We may keep information where required or permitted by law, for example to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our Terms.

**Before you delete:** deletion is permanent, and purchased Virtual Items are not refunded. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies, so request one first if you plan to. Deleting your account does not cancel purchases in your Apple or Google account.
