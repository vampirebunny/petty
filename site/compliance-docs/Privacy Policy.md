## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** \[EFFECTIVE DATE\]

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Innovative Brands LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use Boop Boop ("the App" or "the Service"), a virtual pet game for iOS and Android.

Boop Boop is designed to collect as little as possible. You can play without creating an account. In that case, your pet lives on your device, and we receive only the limited technical data described below. If you create an account to use friends features or to back up your pet, you'll be asked to accept this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### Information You Provide

- **Account Information (optional):** email address, display name, and a sign-in identifier if you use Sign in with Apple or Google. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we receive only a relay address.
- **Age Information:** the age you enter during setup. We use it to confirm you meet our minimum age and to decide whether purchases are available to you. We keep your age range, not your full date of birth. \[CONFIRM\]
- **Pet Data:** your pet's name, species, breed, appearance, attributes, life stage, training progress, tricks, and items you own.
- **Friends Data:** your friend code or username, your friends list, and friend requests you send or accept.
- **Purchase Records:** the item purchased, date, price, and the transaction ID from Apple or Google. We never receive your card or bank details.
- **Communications:** messages you send to our support team, and reports you submit about other users.

#### Information We Collect Automatically

- **Device and App Information:** device model, operating system, app version, language, and IP address.
- **Gameplay and Usage Data:** features used, session length, and in-game events such as feeding, training, and playdates.
- **Diagnostics:** crash reports and performance data.
- **Time Zone and Local Time:** used to match your pet's world to your time of day (for example, a night sky at night). This is read from your device clock. It is not location data.
- **Push Token:** if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send reminders such as "your pet is hungry."

#### What We Do Not Collect

- Precise or approximate location
- Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA or Android Advertising ID). We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

#### Information from Third Parties

- **Sign-In Providers:** if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the basic profile information you allow (name and email, or a relay email).
- **App Stores:** Apple and Google tell us when a purchase succeeds or is refunded so we can deliver or remove the item.

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide the game and keep your pet's state (growth, needs, attributes) running over time
- Create and manage your account and back up your pet across devices
- Power friends features, including friend requests and playdates between friends' pets
- Deliver purchased items and verify transactions
- Apply age rules, including turning off purchases for users under 18
- Send notifications you have allowed
- Respond to support requests and reports
- Improve the game, fix bugs, and develop new features
- Keep the Service secure and prevent fraud and cheating
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not show third-party advertising and do not use your data for personalized ads. \[CONFIRM. If ads are ever added, this section, the store privacy forms, and the consent prompts must all be updated first.\]

We may use aggregated, de-identified data that cannot identify you to understand how the game is played.

### 4. How We Share Your Information

When you add a friend, which requires both of you to accept, that friend can see your display name and your pet's profile: name, breed, appearance, outfits, tricks, and attributes. Pets can only have playdates with friends' pets or with computer-controlled pets. Your pet is not visible to people who are not your friends.

#### Legal Requirements and Business Transfers

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, our users' safety, or to prevent fraud. If Innovative Brands LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred under this Policy.

### 5. Data Stored on Your Device

Without an account, your pet and game progress are stored only on your device. If you delete the App or lose your device, that data cannot be recovered. Creating an account lets us back it up.

### 6. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users and regulators as required by law.

### 7. Data Retention

- **Account and pet data:** until you delete your account
- **Friends data:** until you remove the friend or delete your account
- **Purchase records:** up to 7 years, for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention
- **Diagnostics and usage data:** up to 24 months, then deleted or de-identified
- **Support messages and reports:** up to 2 years after the matter is closed

### 10. Children and Teens

**Minimum Age:** Boop Boop is intended for users 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it and close any associated account.

**Teens (13 to 17):** in-app purchases are not available to users under 18. Teens' pets are visible only to accepted friends, and we do not offer free-text chat between users.

**Parents and Guardians:** if you believe your child under 13 is using Boop Boop, or you have questions about your teen's data, contact \[SUPPORT EMAIL\] with the subject line "Parent Request."

**Age Information:** we rely on the age users enter and on age signals that Apple or Google provide where available. Entering a false age violates our Terms of Service.
