## Community Guidelines

**Last Updated:** \[EFFECTIVE DATE\]

Boop Boop is a friendly place for people 13 and up to raise pets and show them off to friends. These guidelines apply to your display name, your pet's name, and every interaction with other players. They are part of our Terms of Service.

### 1. Be Kind

- Treat friends and their pets with respect
- No bullying, harassment, intimidation, or targeted abuse
- Don't pressure anyone to accept a friend request, or to send you anything

### 2. Keep Names Appropriate

Display names and pet names must not include:

- Hate speech, slurs, or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other characteristic
- Sexual, violent, or drug-related references
- Threats or insults aimed at a person
- Personal information, such as your real full name, phone number, address, school, or social media handles
- Impersonation of another person, a brand, or Boop Boop staff
- Spam, links, or advertising

### 3. Stay Safe

- Keep your personal information private, and don't ask others for theirs
- Only add friends you know
- Never agree to meet someone in person because of the App
- Tell a parent, guardian, or trusted adult if anyone makes you uncomfortable
- Child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form is never allowed. It is reported to law enforcement.

### 4. Play Fair

- No cheats, hacks, mods, bug exploits, or clock manipulation
- No buying, selling, or trading accounts or items outside the App
- One account per person

### 5. Reporting and Blocking

- **Block:** remove someone from your friends and stop all future requests from them, from their profile.
- **Report:** use the report option on a user's profile and choose a reason. Reports are confidential.
- **Serious concerns:** email \[SUPPORT EMAIL\]. If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services first.

We review reports within \[24 HOURS / 1 BUSINESS DAY\] \[CONFIRM what you can staff\].

### 6. Enforcement

Depending on severity and repetition, we may:

- Rename a display name or pet name
- Issue a warning
- Temporarily restrict friends features
- Permanently suspend the account

Severe violations, including child safety violations, threats of violence, or illegal content, result in permanent termination on the first offense.

### 7. Appeals

If you think we made a mistake, email \[SUPPORT EMAIL\] within 30 days. Tell us your username and why you believe the action was wrong. We will respond within 10 business days.

### 8. Updates

We may update these guidelines, and will notify you of significant changes in the App.

## Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your Boop Boop account and its data at any time. This page is for Google Play's data deletion requirement. Publish it at \[DOMAIN\]/delete-account.

### Delete in the App

1. Open Boop Boop and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Account**, then **Delete Account**.
3. Confirm. You'll be signed out, and deletion starts right away.

### Delete Without the App

Email \[PRIVACY EMAIL\] from the address on your account, with the subject line "Delete My Boop Boop Account." If you signed in with Apple's Hide My Email, include your Boop Boop username instead. We may ask you to confirm the request before deleting.

### Playing Without an Account

If you never created an account, your pet is stored only on your device. Deleting the App deletes it. We hold no account data to remove, but you may still email us to delete any diagnostic data linked to your device.

### What Gets Deleted

| Data | What happens | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account details (email, display name, sign-in ID) | Deleted | Within 30 days |
| Pets, progress, and Virtual Items | Deleted, and cannot be recovered | Within 30 days |
| Friends list and friend requests | Deleted; you disappear from friends' lists | Within 30 days |
| Diagnostics and usage data | Deleted or de-identified | Within 30 days |
| Backups | Overwritten on our normal backup cycle | Within 90 days |
| Purchase records | Kept for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention | Up to 7 years |
| Reports involving safety or legal issues | Kept where the law requires it | As required |

**Before you delete:** deletion is permanent. Purchased Virtual Items are not refunded. Request refunds from Apple or Google first if you plan to. Active purchases are not cancelled by deleting your account. Manage them in your Apple or Google account.

### Common Questions

- **I lost my pet after changing phones.** Pets are restored only if you were signed in on your old device. Sign in with the same Apple, Google, or email account on the new one.
- **A purchase didn't arrive.** Go to Settings, then **Restore Purchases**. If it's still missing, email us with your store receipt or order ID.
- **I want a refund.** Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Request refunds at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or from your Google Play order history.
- **Why can't I buy anything?** In-app purchases are available only to users 18 and older, and may be blocked by parental controls on your device.
- **Someone is bothering me.** Block and report them from their profile, then email us if it's serious. See our Community Guidelines.
- **How do I delete my account?** See Account & Data Deletion.
