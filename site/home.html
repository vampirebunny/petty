Made to be loved.

The happy tail wag when you open the app. The sleepy curl-up after a big day of play. The proud moment they finally get a trick right. Boop Boop is all about the little, cozy moments that make a pet feel like family.

They really grow up

Watch your tiny puppy or kitten grow up a little every day, until one day you look back and can't believe how big they got. Older pets stay happy and full of life with good food, walks and lots of love.

A real personality

Some pets are brave, some are cuddly, some are super smart or super fast. Every pet is a little different, and the time you spend together helps bring out their best.

Train them your way

Start with sit, speak and give paw. The more you practice together, the better they listen. Keep at it and you might discover surprise tricks!

Play for real

Throw the ball and watch them run, jump and wiggle with joy. Chase a laser dot with your kitten, toss a frisbee, roll some yarn, and give them a good pat or scratch any time.

Dress them up

Collars, bandanas, coats, sunglasses, hats and boots. Pick their fur color too. Your corgi still looks like a corgi, just extra cute.
